About

The Sovereign Guide

Dr. Jasmine Therese Esguerra is a Doctor of Chiropractic whose clinical practice spans decades of hands-on expertise in optimizing the human biological experience. Her work lives at the intersection of clinical somatics, nervous system regulation, and the philosophy that the human body is a sacred instrument of expression.

As Mrs. Maui, she embodies the bridge between earthly presence and elevated living -- a woman whose personal practice informs her clinical precision and whose warmth creates the conditions for genuine transformation.

She is the sovereign guide, face, and professional authority behind a growing ecosystem of wellness, education, and leadership initiatives designed to elevate how people experience life in their bodies.

The Living Love Modality

A Framework for Embodied Transformation

The Living Love Modality is Dr. Jasmine's signature framework -- a clinical and philosophical approach to reconnecting the human being with their innate capacity for healing, pleasure, and purposeful living. Rooted in somatic principles and refined through years of clinical application, it is both a personal practice and a professional certification pathway.
