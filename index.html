About

The Doctor

Dr. Jasmine Therese Esguerra is a clinical somatic practitioner, chiropractor, and the founding steward of The Living Love Collective — an editorial ecosystem devoted to whole-family generational care through somatic medicine, cultural preservation, and community architecture.

Rooted in Filipino heritage — the Sampaguita lineage of her maternal grandmother — Dr. Jasmine carries forward the wisdom of the islands into a modern clinical framework. Her practice lives at the intersection of ancestral knowing and somatic precision, where the body is not a problem to solve but a story to remember.

Based in Maui, she serves families, practitioners, and leaders who are called to anchor their lineage in power rather than pattern — building not just healthier bodies, but generational architecture that endures.

Cultural Roots

♀

Sampaguita

The Feminine Principle

The national flower of the Philippines — delicate yet enduring. Sampaguita represents the feminine lineage: the mothers, daughters, and grandmothers whose quiet strength anchors entire families. She is the scent of home, the promise of return, the flower that blooms even in the hardest soil.

♂

Bayani

The Masculine Principle

From "bayanihan" — the spirit of communal unity. Bayani represents the masculine lineage: fathers, sons, uncles who carry the weight of protection not through dominance, but through devotion. He is the pillar, the shield-bearer, the one who builds so others may rest.
